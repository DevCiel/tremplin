<h2 id="section-2">Cascade layers</h2>

<p>The full and utilities entries establish this order through root. Individual modules and focused entries can introduce layers in a different sequence; establish the complete order before composing application layers. Optional Tremplin extensions live in custom and are not loaded by the strict core.</p>

<p>Palette output, root tokens, transition classes, drawer rules, keyframes and composition-property registrations include deliberate unlayered output. Directory names alone do not determine layer ownership.</p>

<section class="docs-section" aria-labelledby="section-3">
<h2 id="section-3">Application overrides</h2>

<p>For normal declarations, later layers win before specificity; unlayered application rules beat named layers. Important declarations reverse layer precedence. Utility importance is configured per family/property, not by a global enable-important-utilities switch.</p>

<p>Choose one full or focused entry per stylesheet. Declare application layers after the framework order; do not wrap the whole distribution in another layer unless deliberately changing its contract.</p>
