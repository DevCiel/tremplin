<h2 id="section-0">How it works</h2>

<p>{"Root role tokens such as --trm-primary-bg, --trm-primary-fg and --trm-primary-border supply theme-primary's contextual --trm-theme-* references. Components opt into the corresponding contextual roles. A theme helper assigns variables; surface utilities paint foreground/background/border separately."}</p>

<section class="docs-section" aria-labelledby="section-1">
<h2 id="section-1">Theme colors</h2>

<p>Use exact role names: fg-emphasis, not text-emphasis. Pair solid backgrounds with contrast, and subtle backgrounds with an appropriate foreground; measure contrast rather than assuming every role combination passes.</p>

<section class="docs-section" aria-labelledby="section-2">
<h2 id="section-2">Layer colors</h2>

<p>{"Neutral foregrounds are --trm-fg-body and --trm-fg-1 through 4; backgrounds use --trm-bg-body and --trm-bg-1 through 4. Configure theme-fgs, theme-bgs and theme-borders for neutral roles. Theme-reset clears inherited contextual roles with initial, not unset."}</p>

</section>

<section class="docs-section" aria-labelledby="section-3">
<h2 id="section-3">Theme utility classes</h2>

<p>{"The eight semantic theme-* helpers are accompanied by theme-muted and theme-contrast. There are no theme-light/theme-dark aliases. Native light/dark color mode selection is independent of contextual semantic themes."}</p>

<p>The pinned primary solid button has approximately 3.56:1 white-on-blue normal-text contrast in light mode. Use a measured existing variant or explicitly configured application roles when required; source parity is not a contrast guarantee.</p>
