<h2 id="section-2">{"Behavior, submission and cleanup"}</h2>

<p>{"Use form-range around one labelled form-range-input; every matching wrapper auto-initializes on document ready."}</p>

<p>{"The wrapper receives --trm-range-fill as a ratio, not a percentage. Native bounds supply 0/100 when absent; min>=max makes the adapter ratio zero."}</p>

<p>{"After assigning input.value, call update(). Construction/update and native input/change paths emit changed on the actual input, even when unchanged; it is not a cancelable mutation gate."}</p>

<p>{"Formatter text uses textContent for the bubble only. Tick labels read option.label without formatter; datalist points are snapshotted once, sorted and clamped to positions."}</p>

<p>{"The bubble and ticks are aria-hidden. Native name/value submit the numeric value; formatter does not create aria-valuetext or convert submitted units."}</p>

<p>{"Pinned track gradient is to right and bubble uses left even in RTL; test native thumb versus decoration rather than claiming direction-aware fill. dispose removes generated bubble/ticks/listeners but retains the wrapper fill property."}</p>
