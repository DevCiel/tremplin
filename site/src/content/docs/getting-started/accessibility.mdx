<h2 id="section-0">1. Understand the limits</h2>

<p>The project targets WCAG 2.2 AA and uses automated browser and axe checks. Passing them is not a conformance certification. Manual screen-reader and real-device validation is still pending for final release sign-off.</p>

</section>

<section class="docs-section" aria-labelledby="section-1">
<h2 id="section-1">2. Structure and label your page</h2>

<ol class="docs-steps">
<li>Use landmarks and a logical heading hierarchy, and provide a skip link where navigation repeats.</li>
<li>Use visible labels for controls; placeholders are supplementary hints.</li>
<li>Give informative images useful alt text and hide purely decorative icons.</li>
<li>Use native buttons for actions and anchors for navigation.</li>
</ol>

</section>

<section class="docs-section" aria-labelledby="section-3">
<h2 id="section-3">4. Preserve focus and overlay behavior</h2>

<p>Give Dialog and Drawer a visible title referenced by <code>{"aria-labelledby"}</code>. Test initial focus, Tab and Shift+Tab, Escape, nested surfaces, and restoration to the opener. Never place required form input inside a tooltip.</p>

<section class="docs-section" aria-labelledby="section-4">
<h2 id="section-4">5. Check color, motion, and hidden content</h2>

<p>Do not communicate errors, availability, or status by color alone. Verify text and control contrast after customization. Respect reduced motion and avoid removing focus outlines. Use <code>{".visually-hidden"}</code> for supplementary screen-reader text, not for focusable controls that remain invisible.</p>

<section class="docs-section" aria-labelledby="section-5">
<h2 id="section-5">6. Validate a complete journey</h2>

<p>Read the <a href="https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/quickref/">{"W3C WCAG quick reference"}</a> for success criteria and testing guidance. Automated checks do not replace these manual steps.</p>

<ol class="docs-steps">
<li>Complete the task with the keyboard, including error recovery.</li>
<li>Test at 200% zoom and at a narrow viewport without losing content.</li>
<li>Review announcements and focus with NVDA on Windows; add VoiceOver and other combinations required by your audience.</li>
<li>Run automated checks and record browser, assistive technology, result, and remaining issues.</li>
</ol>

</section>
