<section class="docs-section" aria-labelledby="section-4">
<h2 id="section-4">5. Dependencies and frameworks</h2>

<p>Install <code>{"@floating-ui/dom"}</code> for package-based positioning components such as Menu, Tooltip, and Popover. The browser build includes it. Tremplin Datepicker is implemented locally and does not require Vanilla Calendar Pro.</p>

<section class="docs-section" aria-labelledby="section-5">
<h2 id="section-5">6. Dispose when removing DOM</h2>

<p>In React, Vue, or another renderer, initialize core plugins after mount and dispose before removing their elements. Avoid having both a renderer and a plugin independently own the same state. Experimental adapters remain in the repository but are not exported or installed by the core package; use the core lifecycle directly.</p>

<section class="docs-section" aria-labelledby="section-6">
<h2 id="section-6">7. HTML safety and CSP</h2>

<p>Tooltip and Popover sanitize HTML by default. Keep sanitization enabled; supplying DOM elements or custom sanitizers makes the caller responsible for their safety. Under Trusted Types enforcement, permit the private <code>{"tremplin"}</code> policy.</p>

<div class="docs-callout docs-callout-info">These two directives cover Trusted Types only, not a full CSP. See SECURITY.md in the source package for the complete tested baseline. Provide external application scripts or a deliberate nonce strategy under a strict script policy.</div>

<section class="docs-section" aria-labelledby="section-7">
<h2 id="section-7">8. Without JavaScript</h2>

<p>Typography, grid, tables, cards, and other presentational styles continue to work. Script-driven disclosures and overlays need a usable fallback: keep essential content reachable and do not make critical tasks depend on a hidden, uninitialized panel.</p>
