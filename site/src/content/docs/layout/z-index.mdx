<section class="docs-section" aria-labelledby="section-0">
<h2 id="section-0">Stacking order</h2>

<p>A z-index value is compared within a stacking context, not across the entire document. Positioning, transforms, opacity, isolation, and other properties can create new contexts. Increasing an arbitrary number may not bring a child above a sibling context.</p>

</section>

<section class="docs-section" aria-labelledby="section-2">
<h2 id="section-2">Native top layer</h2>

<p>Native modal dialogs and browser popovers can enter the browser’s top layer. Ordinary z-index values cannot place regular page content above a top-layer modal. Test Dialog, Drawer, Menu, Tooltip, and nested overlays together before changing their layer rules.</p>

</section>
